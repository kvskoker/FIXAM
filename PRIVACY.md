# Privacy Policy

**Fixam — Civic Infrastructure Reporting Platform**
**Effective date: April 2026**

---

## 1. Who We Are

Fixam is a civic technology platform built for Sierra Leone. It enables citizens to report local infrastructure problems — broken roads, faulty streetlights, water supply failures, and more — via WhatsApp. Reports are aggregated and shared with government agencies to help prioritize and track repairs.

**Data Controller:**
Fixam
Freetown, Sierra Leone
📧 privacy@fixam.sl
🌐 https://fixam.sl

---

## 2. Your Consent

We ask for your explicit consent before creating your account. When you first message Fixam on WhatsApp, you will receive a privacy notice and must reply **YES** before any personal data is stored.

You may withdraw consent at any time by sending **DELETE MY DATA** to our WhatsApp number. This permanently deletes your account and all associated data.

---

## 3. Data We Collect

| Data | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| Phone number | Account identification and WhatsApp communication |
| Name | Personalising your experience |
| GPS location | Pinpointing where infrastructure problems are reported |
| Photos and images | Documenting infrastructure issues visually |
| Voice recordings | Accessibility for users who prefer voice reporting |
| Community points and votes | Tracking civic engagement and prioritising issues |
| Conversation state | Maintaining your session between messages |
| Message logs | Debugging, moderation, and service quality |

We do **not** collect financial data, national ID numbers, or health data.

---

## 4. Legal Basis for Processing

- **Consent** — You explicitly agree before registration. You may withdraw at any time.
- **Legitimate interest** — To improve public infrastructure in Sierra Leone through civic engagement.
- **Legal obligation** — Where required by Sierra Leone law.

---

## 5. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to:

- Receive, validate, and route your infrastructure reports to the relevant government agencies
- Display anonymised, aggregated reports to government and the public on our dashboard
- Enable community voting to prioritise issues
- Send you updates on reports you submitted
- Prevent abuse and spam through moderation
- Improve the Fixam platform

We **never**:
- Sell your data to third parties
- Use your data for advertising
- Share personally identifiable information with government without your consent, except as required by law

---

## 6. Data Sharing

| Recipient | What is shared | Why |
|-----------|---------------|-----|
| Sierra Leone government agencies (e.g. SLRA, EDSA, FCC, GUMA) | Anonymised, aggregated report data | Infrastructure repair prioritisation |
| Cloud hosting provider | Encrypted account and report data | Platform infrastructure |
| Meta (WhatsApp Business API) | Messages you send and receive | Communication delivery |

All third-party service providers are contractually bound to protect your data and may not use it for their own purposes.

---

## 7. Data Retention

| Data | Retention |
|------|-----------|
| Active account data | Until you delete your account |
| Reports (anonymised after deletion) | Indefinitely — civic infrastructure data is preserved without personal linkage |
| Message logs | 90 days |
| Deleted account personal data | Permanently deleted within 30 days |

## 8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

- **Access** — See all data we hold about you
- **Correction** — Ask us to correct inaccurate information
- **Deletion** — Permanently delete your account and personal data
- **Portability** — Receive a summary of your data

### How to exercise your rights via WhatsApp

| What you want | Message to send |
|---------------|----------------|
| See your data summary | `MY DATA` |
| Delete your account permanently | `DELETE MY DATA` |
| Update your name | `UPDATE NAME [your new name]` |
| Contact support | `HELP` |

You can also email **privacy@fixam.sl** and we will respond within 14 days.

---

## 9. Security

We protect your data using:

- **bcrypt password hashing** (cost factor 12) for secure credential storage
- **HTTPS** encryption for all data in transit
- **Restricted CORS** so our API only accepts requests from authorised domains
- **Rate limiting** to prevent abuse
- **Role-based access controls** limiting staff access to personal data

To report a security vulnerability, see our [Security Policy](./SECURITY.md).
